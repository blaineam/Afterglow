## description
Put your iPhone in a viewer and step inside. Afterglow XR turns the phone in your pocket into a headset with real head tracking, your own hands as the controller, and your room around you.

YOUR ROOM, YOUR HANDS
Move and lean with six degrees of freedom. Your room shows through the cameras, with depth, so glass windows hang in it and stay where you put them. Pinch to select, grab to move, point to aim. No controllers, nothing to charge.

YOUR LIBRARY IN THREE DIMENSIONS
Spatial photos and videos play back in stereo. Panoramas wrap around you. 360 photos become the room itself. 180 and 360 video, side by side and top and bottom. Browse your Photos library, or your own folders in Files, including folders from cloud storage apps, and read them in place.

A WEB BROWSER YOU CAN REACH INTO
Tabs, bookmarks, history, saved logins and private browsing, with text sized to read through a lens. Pages built with WebXR open immersive VR and AR sessions, with your hands as the input, and an Exit button at your feet whenever you want to leave.

TWENTY-FOUR PLACES TO BE
Sit in a Japanese garden, a grand library or a starship cabin. Twenty-four photospheres are built in, and your own 360 photos join them. The one you pick is still there next time.

TWELVE GAMES
Paint glowing ribbons in the air. Putt eighteen holes of mini golf at your feet. Ride a coaster on a course that is new every time. Blast blobs with a punch, vacuum your real floor, wake a toy box, step through a portal, and play rhythm to your own music. Stack a tower on your real table, grow a glowing garden, keep a ball in orbit with your palms, and drive a neon road by looking where you want to go.

YOUR MUSIC, YOUR WATCH, YOUR CONTROLLER
Control Apple Music from inside. Use Apple Watch as a clicker with Double Tap and the Digital Crown, or pair a game controller.

TUNED FOR COMFORT
Head smoothing you can adjust, 3DoF mode on the ultra-wide camera, and adjustable render scale and frame rate.

WHAT YOU NEED
A phone viewer you can put your iPhone in. Afterglow XR is tuned for iPhone 18 Pro Max, and the plans for a viewer you can print yourself are free. Moving through your room, surfaces and depth use the LiDAR scanner on iPhone Pro models. No viewer to hand? Phone view runs it all in your hand.

Free, with no account and no ads. It is open source, and it does not collect your data: your camera, your hands and your voice stay on your iPhone.

## review_notes
Afterglow XR is a viewer app: the screen normally shows two pictures, one for each eye, for an iPhone held in a headset. NO VIEWER IS NEEDED TO REVIEW IT, using the steps below. There is no account, sign-in, purchase or server of ours.

FASTEST REVIEW PATH (about 3 minutes, in landscape):
1. Launch and allow the Camera. It is used for head and hand tracking and to show the room. You will see two pictures side by side.
2. Tap the gear icon in the top-right corner and check PHONE VIEW. The app now shows one full-screen picture you can move around by moving the iPhone.
3. In Phone view a small cursor sits at the centre of the screen. Aim by moving the iPhone, and tap anywhere to select what the cursor is on. Pinching your thumb and index finger in front of the back camera does the same.
4. The launcher window has Browser, Media, Files, Games, Places, Music, Settings and Lock. Close a window with its red button. Look down to find the dock.
5. Games: open Games and pick Tower, then tap to drop slabs. Other good ones without a viewer: Mini golf, Glow garden, Skyline drive (steer by turning). The controls window holds Restart and Stop.
6. Places: pick a photosphere to replace the surroundings. Browser: open any web page; pages built with WebXR offer an Enter VR or Start AR button, and an Exit VR button sits at your feet in the session (a double tap on the back of the phone also leaves).
7. Media asks for Photos access, and Music for Apple Music access, only when opened.

PERMISSIONS: Camera (tracking and passthrough; images stay on the device). Photos and Apple Music only when those windows are opened. Microphone and Speech Recognition only when dictating into the browser or saying a passcode; a spoken passcode is recognised on device only and never logged.

NETWORK: the app itself contacts no server of ours and collects no data. The browser loads the pages the user opens. The rhythm game may look up the tempo of the current song with a public music tempo service, sending only the song's title and artist. WebXR pages receive the head pose during an immersive session. They receive hand positions only after the user allows it for that site, just this time or always, in a prompt in the browser.

DEVICE SUPPORT: iPhone only, and tuned for iPhone 18 Pro Max. Features that need LiDAR (surfaces, depth passthrough) are skipped on iPhones without it. The optional Apple Watch app is a clicker, and needs the iPhone app.
